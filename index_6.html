About

I'm a Computer Engineer with a strong interest in Data Engineering, AI and Machine Learning. Today I work as a Data Engineer at Target Reply, where I design, migrate and maintain data pipelines for large enterprise clients such as Amplifon and Fastweb.

My day-to-day sits between SQL and the business: modelling data in dbt, orchestrating it with Airflow, running it on BigQuery and GCP, and making sure the KPIs that reach Power BI are consistent and trustworthy.

Before joining Reply I worked on machine learning at IRCCS Carlo Besta, classifying ALS subtypes from high-dimensional molecular data. That work became my M.Sc. thesis and is now being prepared for publication.

I care about clean models, tested pipelines and numbers people can trust.

Research

paper in progressM.Sc. thesis · Politecnico di Milano × IRCCS Carlo Besta · 2025

An integrative bioinformatics and machine learning approach for non-coding RNA-based signatures in Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis

A. Cacciatore et al. · manuscript in preparation

Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis is highly heterogeneous, and reliable molecular markers of its subtypes are still missing. This work combines the analysis of miRNA, lncRNA and cytokine profiles with machine learning to identify non-coding RNA signatures able to discriminate ALS subtypes, with a pipeline built for small-sample, high-dimensional data.
